Privacy Policy

Audio & video stay on your device · Not uploaded to our servers
Effective date: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

AV Merge Master (the “App”) is an on-device video and audio studio for iPhone. Core features include merging video and audio clips, multitrack mixing, trimming and transitions, toolbox effects, an optional recorder with speech-to-text, local drafts, and export to formats such as MP4 or M4A.

This policy explains how we treat information related to you. Processing is primarily on-device: recordings, imported media copies, merge projects, exports, and settings are stored on your device by default. We do not operate a backend server to centrally store your media, and we do not use that content for advertising or user profiling. By using the App, you acknowledge this policy.

2. Information We Handle

The App does not upload your personal identity or finished media to developer-owned servers to identify who you are. In normal use, the following data remains on your device (or is shared via Apple’s App Group between the main App and extensions such as widgets or Live Activities on the same device—still without passing through our servers):

  • Projects and media: clips you record or import, merge order, waveforms or caches, drafts, and export settings—used for editing, preview, and export.
  • Speech-to-text (if used): transcription uses Apple’s speech recognition; processing stays within your device and Apple’s systems. We do not build a separate voice profile database.
  • Exports (if used): files such as MP4, M4A, or optional poster PDFs are generated locally when you export. Content is sent elsewhere only if you choose system Share or a similar action.
  • Settings and Pro status: language, appearance, and purchase entitlement caches stored with system APIs (including UserDefaults and App Group).

We do not collect advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking, and we do not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. System Permissions

The App requests system permissions only when needed for a feature. You can change them anytime in Settings → AV Merge Master:

  • Microphone: to record audio; audio is captured only when you start recording.
  • Speech Recognition: to transcribe recordings when you use that feature; follow the system prompt to grant access.
  • Photo Library: to pick videos or media you choose, or to save exported results to Photos when you request it.
  • Files: to import from the Files app or save results to a location you choose.

Denying a permission may disable the related feature; other features remain available.

4. Apple In-App Purchases (Pro)

The App may offer an optional Pro upgrade via the App Store (product type and identifiers follow App Store Connect and in-app display). Price, tax, and wording follow your regional App Store.

Payment and receipts are handled by Apple; we never receive your card number or full payment credentials. Purchase and restore use your Apple Account and StoreKit. We update local Pro status from Apple transaction or entitlement data on your device. Prior subscriptions or one-time purchases may still unlock access when Apple’s rules allow, based on actual transaction history.

For refunds, billing, or Family Sharing, use Apple Support or your Apple Account settings.

5. Widgets and Extensions (If Used)

If you add a Home Screen widget or use related extensions (such as a recorder Live Activity), the extension and main App may read a small amount of configuration or shortcut data via Apple’s App Group locally on your device. That data is not uploaded to developer servers.

6. Storage and Security

User content and project data are stored mainly in the App sandbox. Uninstalling the App removes sandbox data (unless the system retains something separately). Keep your device and passcode secure. We cannot remotely access the data on your device.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

Except where required by law or a lawful request from a court or regulator, we do not sell or rent your personal information. Because the App does not maintain a centralized identifiable user profile, there is typically no “user database” shared with third parties.

When you use the system Share sheet, AirDrop, Mail, or open content in another app, that content leaves the App by your action and is handled under the recipient’s policies.

8. Third Parties and Network Access

The App does not integrate third-party SDKs for ads or tracking. Network use related to Apple mainly includes StoreKit (purchase verification / restore) and links you open yourself (such as this privacy policy or the user agreement). Core merge and recording features can work offline; purchase verification or speech recognition may still require network access depending on system behavior.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. If a parent or guardian believes a child provided information without consent, contact us below and we will help resolve it.

10. Your Choices

You can control local data by deleting in-app content, uninstalling the App, or turning off system permissions. Contact us with questions about this policy or App Store review.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when features or legal requirements change, and we will revise the effective date on this page. For material changes, we will provide reasonable notice where appropriate. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
