SwipeBye

Privacy Policy

On-device processing · No ads · No tracking

Effective date: October 8, 2026

In short: SwipeBye organizes and cleans up your photos on your device.

We do not collect, upload, sell, or share your photos, videos, albums, or cleanup history. We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

SwipeBye ("the App") is a photo cleanup utility for iPhone. It helps you review photos with swipe gestures, confirm deletions in batches, tidy albums, compress large media on device, and unlock optional Pro cleanup tools.

Deletion and album changes only happen after you confirm them. The App never uploads your photo library to developer-controlled servers.

1. Who We Are

The App is published under the name SwipeBye with bundle identifier com.nick.phonecleanup. For privacy questions, see Section 14 (Contact).

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal data through the App. The App does not require an account, sign-in, email registration, or phone number to operate.

We do not operate developer-controlled servers that receive your photos, videos, Live Photos, screenshots, album names, cleanup selections, compression results, or review progress for processing, storage, advertising, analytics, or model training.

3. Photos, Videos, and Library Content

When you grant Photos access, the App reads media from your on-device photo library only to provide the features you request, such as:

  • swipe-based review and organization sessions
  • batch confirmation for delete or favorite candidates
  • album browsing and album membership edits you approve
  • similar, large, screenshot, and video cleanup queues
  • on-device image and video compression
  • local cleanup statistics and achievements

Your photo and video content stays on your device. Exported compressed copies are saved only when you choose to save them back to Photos.

4. On-Device Processing

Cleanup analysis, similarity grouping, size estimates, compression, and progress tracking run on your device.

  • Processing happens locally using Apple frameworks such as Photos, Vision where needed, and AVFoundation.
  • Results are used only to power the current cleanup workflow.
  • They are not uploaded to developer servers.
  • They are not used to identify you personally beyond organizing your own library.

If a photo includes location metadata and you use location-based organization, the App may reverse-geocode coordinates on device through Apple location services so it can show a place name. That lookup is used only for local organization labels and is not sent to developer servers.

5. App Permissions

The App requests system permissions only when needed:

  • Photos (read): to display and review items in your library for cleanup and album organization.
  • Photos (add/save): to apply favorites, album edits, and save compressed copies when you ask.

The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or tracking permission. You can deny or revoke Photos access at any time in iOS Settings. Some features will not work without Photos access.

6. Local Storage on Your Device

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

  • app settings and preferences such as language, theme, gestures, and review options
  • onboarding completion state
  • local review progress and cleanup statistics
  • purchase entitlement state needed to unlock Pro features
  • limited widget configuration shared through Apple App Group between the main App and Home Screen widgets, if widgets are used

This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to developer servers. You can remove most local App data by deleting the App. Photos you keep, delete, or export remain under your control in the Photos app.

7. In-App Purchases

SwipeBye offers optional Pro upgrades processed by Apple through StoreKit. Core swipe cleanup remains available without purchase. Apple handles payment, taxes, refunds, and transaction records.

We do not receive or store your credit card number, full billing details, Apple ID password, or Apple ID credentials. The App only keeps the local entitlement state needed to unlock purchased features and to restore purchases.

For refunds or billing issues, please use Apple standard App Store support channels.

8. Third-Party Services, Advertising, and Tracking

The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, social login SDKs, or cross-app tracking tools.

According to our App Store privacy label and privacy manifest, the App does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

If you tap a link that opens the App Store, Apple Photos, Mail, Safari, or another Apple system feature, that external service is governed by Apple's or the third party's own policies.

9. Sharing, Sale, and Disclosure

Because we do not collect personal data through the App, we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising or profiling.

We may disclose information only if:

  • you voluntarily send it to us, for example in a support email,
  • we need it to respond to your support request, or
  • disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.

10. International Users

The App is designed to process content locally on your device. Because we do not collect personal data through the App, there is no routine transfer of your photos or personal information to developer servers in other countries.

If you contact us by email, your message may be stored on the email systems you and we use to communicate.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the App. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

12. Your Choices and Rights

  • Deny or revoke Photos permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Delete the App to remove local App preferences and cached cleanup state.
  • Manage, keep, or delete photos directly in the Photos app.
  • Contact us to request deletion of information you previously sent by email.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under laws such as GDPR or CCPA. Because the App does not collect personal data for routine operation, many data-access requests may not apply. We will still try to help with reasonable requests related to support correspondence.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes, when our practices change, or when legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page shows the latest version. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
