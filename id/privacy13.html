Lag Rescue Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026
App: Lag Rescue
Bundle ID: com.nickgao.networken

Lag Rescue (“we,” “us,” or “the app”) is a network utility for iPhone that helps you measure internet speed, diagnose lag, and run connection tools such as Ping, DNS lookup, traceroute, LAN scan, bufferbloat tests, and related diagnostics. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, what stays on your device, and when network requests leave your device.

1. Summary

  • No account is required.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • We do not operate a developer backend that stores your test history or personal profile.
  • Speed results, settings, and similar data are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share them.
  • When you run a test or lookup, your device contacts public network endpoints needed for that feature (for example Cloudflare speed endpoints or public IP / DNS services). Those providers may see technical request data under their own policies.

2. Information processed on your device

Lag Rescue processes information on your device to provide the features you use. Depending on the feature, this may include:

  • Connection details: Wi‑Fi or cellular status, SSID (when iOS allows), carrier name, local IP address, and public IP details shown in the app.
  • Measurement results: download and upload throughput, latency, jitter, packet loss, bufferbloat indicators, network quality score, traceroute hops, DNS answers, certificate details, and port-check results.
  • Local network scan results: responsive device IPs and hostnames discovered on the network you choose to scan.
  • App settings and history: selected test server and mode, broadband plan settings you enter, free-test usage count, purchase unlock state cache, onboarding completion, and speed-test history.
  • Images you create: posters or QR images you choose to save or share.

This information is used only to show results to you, keep history on device, and operate in-app purchases through Apple. It is not uploaded to Lag Rescue developer servers, because the app does not provide a cloud sync or account service.

3. Information we do not collect

Lag Rescue does not:

  • create user accounts or ask for your name, email, or password inside the app;
  • sell personal information;
  • show third-party ads;
  • use third-party analytics or advertising identifiers for tracking across apps or websites;
  • read your contacts, photos library contents (beyond saving an image you explicitly export), microphone, camera, or Health data.

4. Network requests to third-party endpoints

To measure your connection, Lag Rescue sends technical requests from your device to endpoints required by the feature you start. Examples include:

  • speed-test download/upload endpoints (default: Cloudflare; optional China mirror download hosts if you select them);
  • public IP / ISP lookup services;
  • DNS resolvers and hosts you enter for Ping, traceroute, certificate check, or port check;
  • short connectivity and stability probes used by the 30-Second Checkup and other diagnostics.

Those third parties may process technical data such as your IP address, request timing, and standard server logs under their own privacy policies. Lag Rescue does not control those third-party systems. Network requests happen only when you start the related feature (or when an optional auto server-selection check runs before a speed test you start).

5. Permissions

iOS may ask for the following permissions. Each is optional and used only for the stated purpose:

  • Local Network: to discover devices on your current Wi‑Fi network when you use LAN Scan or similar local tools.
  • Location (When In Use): only because iOS may require location permission to display the current Wi‑Fi network name (SSID). Lag Rescue does not use continuous background location tracking and does not build a location history for advertising.
  • Photo Library (Add Only): only when you choose to save a generated speed-test poster or QR image to Photos.

You can change or revoke these permissions in iOS Settings.

6. Purchases

Lag Rescue Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase (product ID com.nickgao.networken.upgrade). It is not a subscription and does not auto-renew. Payment processing, billing, refunds, family sharing (if enabled by Apple), and Restore Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit / your Apple ID. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal information and do not share personal information with advertisers or analytics providers. Sharing occurs only in these limited cases:

  • with third-party test or lookup endpoints as needed to perform a feature you start (see Section 4);
  • with Apple for in-app purchase processing;
  • when you use the system Share sheet or save/export a result yourself;
  • if required by law or to protect rights and safety, to the extent applicable.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because most app data stays on your device, you control retention. You can delete speed-test history in the app (where available) or remove remaining local app data by deleting Lag Rescue from your device. Purchase records remain associated with your Apple ID according to Apple’s policies.

9. International users and your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive information about personal data, or to object to certain processing. Because Lag Rescue does not maintain user accounts or a developer-side database of your tests, most data is already under your device control. For privacy questions or requests, contact us using the email below. We will respond as required by applicable law.

10. Children’s privacy

Lag Rescue is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a channel we control, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

11. Security

We design Lag Rescue to minimize data leaving your device. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your device and Apple ID secure.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Continued use of Lag Rescue after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
