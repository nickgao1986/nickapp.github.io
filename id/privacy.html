隐私概要

PasteDeck 不会收集、不会上传你的剪贴板内容、常用语或使用数据。历史记录、短语与设置仅保存在你的设备本地（应用沙盒与 App Group 容器）。应用不包含分析 SDK、广告 SDK，也不会跨 App 追踪。

零内容上传 本地存储 无广告追踪

我们是谁

PasteDeck（「本应用」）是一款剪贴板历史、常用语管理与自定义键盘工具，发布于 App Store，主应用 Bundle ID 为 com.nickgao.pastedeck，键盘扩展为 com.nickgao.pastedeck.keyboard。

我们收集哪些信息

日常使用中，我们不通过本应用收集个人数据。无需注册账号，也无需提供邮箱或手机号即可使用核心功能。

我们不运营接收剪贴板内容、常用语、击键记录或使用统计的开发者服务器。

本地数据如何使用

  • 剪贴板历史：当你回到本应用前台，且已开启「自动记录」时，应用会读取系统剪贴板文本并保存在本地，用于展示历史与一键再复制。iOS 可能弹出系统「允许粘贴」确认，该弹窗由系统控制。
  • 常用语：你主动添加的短语与分类仅保存在本地，用于一键复制与键盘输入。
  • 键盘扩展：在系统设置中开启「允许完全访问」后，键盘才能读取上述本地共享数据并在输入框中展示。键盘默认不会直读系统剪贴板（可在设置中自行开启）。键盘不会记录击键，也不会把内容发送到网络。
  • 设置与偏好：语言、主题色、键盘行为等偏好保存在本机 UserDefaults / App Group 中。
  • 购买状态：Pro 权益状态由 StoreKit 在本地维护，用于解锁容量上限。

网络与第三方

剪贴板与常用语内容本身不会因本应用功能而离开设备。以下情况可能使用网络，且由 Apple 或你主动触发：

  • 通过 App Store / StoreKit 购买或恢复 PasteDeck Pro（商品 ID：com.nickgao.pastedeck.pro）
  • 打开本页隐私政策、用户协议，或前往 App Store 评分 / 其他应用页面
  • 通过「设置 → 反馈问题」发送邮件

支付由 Apple 处理，我们不获取也不存储信用卡号、账单详情或 Apple ID 密码。

数据删除

  • 可在应用内删除单条历史、清空历史，或删除常用语与分类。
  • 可在设置中关闭自动记录剪贴板。
  • 卸载应用将删除应用沙盒与相关本地数据（已导出到其他 App 的内容除外）。

儿童隐私

本应用面向一般用户。我们不会通过本应用故意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。若你认为儿童通过邮件向我们发送了个人信息，请联系我们，我们会合理删除。

政策变更

我们可能随产品或法律要求更新本政策。页面顶部的生效日期表示最新版本。继续使用本应用即表示你了解更新后的政策。

Privacy Summary

PasteDeck does not collect or upload your clipboard contents, phrases, or usage data. History, phrases, and settings stay on your device (app sandbox and App Group container). There are no analytics SDKs, ad SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

No content upload On-device storage No ads / tracking

Who We Are

PasteDeck ("the App") is a clipboard history, phrase manager, and custom keyboard utility on the App Store. The main app bundle ID is com.nickgao.pastedeck; the keyboard extension is com.nickgao.pastedeck.keyboard.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal data through the App for routine operation. No account, email, or phone number is required for core features.

We do not operate developer servers that receive clipboard text, phrases, keystrokes, or analytics.

How Local Data Is Used

  • Clipboard history: When you return the App to the foreground and Auto-Capture is on, the App may read system clipboard text and store it locally for history and re-copy. iOS may show its standard paste-permission alert.
  • Phrases: Phrases and categories you add are stored locally for one-tap copy and keyboard input.
  • Keyboard extension: After you enable "Allow Full Access," the keyboard can read the local shared data above. Direct pasteboard reading in the keyboard is off by default and optional. The keyboard does not log keystrokes and does not send content over the network.
  • Settings: Language, theme, and keyboard preferences are stored in on-device UserDefaults / App Group storage.
  • Purchases: Pro entitlement state is maintained locally via StoreKit to unlock capacity limits.

Network and Third Parties

Clipboard and phrase content do not leave your device for PasteDeck features. Network use may occur only when Apple or you initiate it, for example:

  • Buying or restoring PasteDeck Pro via the App Store / StoreKit (product ID: com.nickgao.pastedeck.pro)
  • Opening this privacy policy, terms, App Store rating pages, or other app links
  • Sending feedback mail from Settings → Feedback

Payments are handled by Apple. We do not receive or store card numbers, full billing details, or Apple ID credentials.

Data Deletion

  • Delete individual history items, clear history, or remove phrases/categories inside the App.
  • Turn off Auto-Capture in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the App removes sandbox and related local data (except content you exported elsewhere).

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the App. If you believe a child sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy when the product or legal requirements change. The effective date above shows the latest version. Continued use means you understand the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, use Settings → Feedback in the App, or email youjiancau@sina.com.
