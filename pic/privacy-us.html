Photo Squeeze

Privacy Policy · Effective August 17, 2026

1. Overview

Photo Squeeze is a photo and video compression utility. Media processing happens on your device. We do not collect, upload, sell, or use your photos, videos, or personal information for tracking or advertising.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Identity information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers, location data, or advertising profiles
  • Photo or video contents beyond temporary on-device processing
  • Analytics or usage data via third-party SDKs

3. Permissions

  • Photo Library (read) — to select photos or videos you choose to compress, convert, or organize
  • Photo Library (add) — to save compressed or converted copies back to your library
  • Camera — only when you choose to capture a photo or video for editing
  • Microphone — only when you record a video with sound

Captured and processed media stays on your device unless you share it through an iOS share sheet destination you choose.

4. On-Device Processing

Compression, format conversion, duplicate review, and related features run locally on your iPhone or iPad. Your media is not uploaded to developer servers.

5. Deleting Originals

If you choose to remove original photos after saving compressed or converted copies, iOS moves those originals to the Photos app’s Recently Deleted album. Apple normally allows recovery there for up to 30 days.

6. Purchases

Pro upgrades use Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Apple processes payment; we never see your payment card details. We only receive entitlement status needed to unlock Pro features. Use Restore Purchases if entitlements are missing after reinstall.

7. Diagnostics and Tracking

The app does not include third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs. Apple may process App Store purchase and diagnostic information under Apple’s own privacy policy and your device settings.

8. Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
