Phone Cleanup Master

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Short version: Phone Cleanup Master analyzes and processes your photos and videos entirely on your device.

We do not collect, upload, sell, share, or track personal data from the app. We do not use third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

Phone Cleanup Master is a utilities app that helps you reclaim storage and tidy your photo library. Features may include finding similar photos, duplicates, blurry shots, screenshots, and Live Photos; swipe-to-clean and date-based cleanup; photo and video compression; HEIC / JPEG / PNG format conversion; album organization; deep clean for large files and app cache; and storage overview. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal data from the app. Your photos, videos, scan results (such as duplicate groups, blurry-photo flags, and screenshot categories), compression history, album organization choices, app preferences, and local usage counters are processed and stored on your device unless you choose to export or share them yourself.

The app does not require an account, does not provide developer-operated cloud processing, and does not send your photos or videos to developer servers for analysis, compression, model training, advertising, or analytics.

2. Photos, Videos, and Cleanup Content

When you grant Photo Library access, Phone Cleanup Master may scan your library on device to identify similar photos, duplicates, blurry shots, screenshots, Live Photos, large files, and recently imported items.

Cleanup actions that delete or modify media happen only after you confirm them (or, if you turn on an optional automatic cleanup rule, according to that rule until you turn it off). Deleted items typically move to the system Recently Deleted album and can be restored within about 30 days under Apple’s standard Photos behavior. Compressed or converted files are saved only when you choose to save or export them.

3. On-Device Analysis

Duplicate detection, blur detection, screenshot recognition, similar-photo grouping, and related recommendations use on-device image analysis (for example perceptual hashing and clarity checks). These computations run locally. Intermediate results (such as an analysis cache) may be stored on your device to speed up later scans. Image content is not transmitted off device for these operations.

4. App Permissions

Phone Cleanup Master requests system permissions only when needed for features:

  • Photo Library: scan, preview, organize, compress, convert, and clean up photos and videos. Full access enables the complete feature set; Limited Photos access restricts work to items you select.
  • Photo Library Add: save compressed photos and converted videos back to your library.
  • Other permissions (only if a feature needs them): for example Camera, Microphone, or Face ID / Touch ID if shown by the system for a specific optional feature. The app does not collect biometric templates; Face ID / Touch ID verification is handled by the system.

You can manage or revoke permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Local Storage and Cache

The app may store settings, preferences, feature counters, purchase entitlement state, cleanup history, and an analysis cache on your device using Apple-provided local storage. This information keeps the app working as expected and is not sent to developer servers.

You can clear analysis cache from within the app where that option is provided, or remove local app data by deleting the app. Items you deleted from Photos still follow Apple’s Recently Deleted rules.

6. In-App Purchases

Phone Cleanup Master Pro is an optional one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase processed by Apple StoreKit. It is not an auto-renewing subscription.

Apple handles payment, taxes, refunds, and transaction records. We do not receive or store your credit card number, full billing details, Apple ID password, or Apple ID credentials. You can restore eligible purchases in the app using Restore Purchases.

7. Support Emails

If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address and the information you include in your message. We use that only to respond, troubleshoot, and keep support records. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

8. Third-Party SDKs, Advertising, and Tracking

Phone Cleanup Master does not use third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or third-party login systems. We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

9. Sharing, Sale, and Disclosure

Because we do not collect personal data from the app, we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, targeted advertising, or profiling. We may disclose information only if you provide it directly to us (for example in a support email), if needed to respond to your request, or if required by applicable law.

10. Children’s Privacy

Phone Cleanup Master is a general-audience utility. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through support email, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

11. Your Choices

  • Deny or revoke Photo Library (and other) permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Turn off any automatic cleanup rule in the app.
  • Clear the analysis cache in the app when that option is available.
  • Delete local app data by deleting the app.
  • Review and restore items from Recently Deleted in the Photos app within Apple’s retention period.
  • Contact us to request deletion of support emails you previously sent.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, our practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be available from the app and from the App Store privacy policy link.
