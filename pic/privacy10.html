Days Count Privacy Policy

Effective: August 16, 2026
Last updated: August 16, 2026
App: Days Count (Bundle ID: com.nickgao.counten)

Days Count is built privacy first. Event titles, dates, notes, custom icons, and background photos stay on your device by default. We do not require an account, and we do not operate a developer server that receives your countdown content. Network processing happens only when you choose related features, such as optional iCloud sync, in-app purchases, optional analytics (Umeng+), or optional AI text polishing (Qwen).

1. Introduction

Days Count helps you track countdowns and count-ups for important dates, with list and calendar views, local reminders, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, holiday suggestions, share cards, and optional voice input, AI polishing, analytics, iCloud sync, and a one-time Pro unlock.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app. By continuing to use Days Count, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the app and uninstall it. Related terms are described in the Terms of Use.

2. Information You Create

The developer does not operate a backend for collecting your event content and does not require an app account. The following information is used mainly to provide features on your device:

  • Events and content (on device): titles, notes, target dates, categories, repeat rules, lunar settings, countdown or count-up mode, reminder rules, pin and favorite state, custom icons, colors, and background photo files stored locally in the app sandbox, UserDefaults, and Documents.
  • App settings (on device): nickname, appearance, onboarding state, iCloud sync toggle, local Pro unlock status, and related preferences.
  • Widget snapshots (on-device App Group): limited countdown snapshots may be shared with widgets through App Group group.com.nickgao.counten, remaining on your device and Apple's local shared container.

We do not use this local content for advertising profiles, cross-app tracking, or sale to third parties.

3. Optional Network Features

These features send data over the network only when you use or enable them.

  • Optional iCloud sync (free): If you turn sync on, supported event metadata and settings may sync through Apple CloudKit to your private iCloud database (container: iCloud.com.nickgao.counten) across your Apple devices. Event background photos remain on device and are not uploaded as photo assets to a developer server. Apple processes iCloud data under Apple's terms and privacy policy.
  • In-app purchases: Days Count Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple StoreKit / App Store. We do not receive your payment-card details. The app stores unlock status locally and supports Restore Purchases for the same Apple Account. Product ID: com.nickgao.counten.upgrade.
  • Sharing and external links: When you share a card, leave a review, or open web pages, the system share sheet, App Store, or browser may be used under their own rules.
  • Optional AI text polishing (Qwen / Alibaba Cloud Model Studio): Only if you tap an AI polish action and confirm sending content to the cloud will Days Count send the title and note text you are currently editing to Alibaba Cloud's Qwen service to suggest clearer wording. We do not upload recordings, photos, your full photo library, contacts, or location for this feature, and we do not batch-upload your event list. Whether you keep the suggestion is up to you. You can avoid this feature entirely.
  • Optional analytics (Umeng+): Only after you explicitly allow analytics does the app initialize Umeng+ to understand product usage (for example screen views, button taps, purchase flow results, and usage counts for voice or AI features), along with device identifiers needed for analytics. Analytics does not upload full event titles, note content, photos, or recordings. If you choose not to allow analytics, Umeng is not initialized and core local features still work.

4. Permissions You Choose

  • Contacts (optional import): Used only when you import birthdays. Days Count may read names and birthday fields you select. Contacts are not uploaded to a developer server.
  • Calendar (optional import): Used only when you import calendar events you choose. Your calendar is not batch-uploaded.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: Used for optional dictation of titles or notes. Audio is processed with Apple speech capabilities. Days Count does not upload recording files to a developer server or to Qwen. If the system requires network speech processing on some devices or settings, Apple's rules apply. You can revoke microphone or speech permissions in Settings.
  • Photos: Days Count uses the system photo picker for images you explicitly choose as backgrounds or share cards. It does not scan your full library.
  • Notifications: Delivers local reminders you schedule. Reminder content stays on device.
  • Face ID or Touch ID: Used to unlock the app or hidden events. Biometric templates are handled by iOS and are never available to Days Count.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • You can edit or delete events and content in the app. Uninstalling usually removes local sandbox data.
  • Deleting local data does not cancel App Store purchases. Reinstall and use Restore Purchases to recover eligible Pro access.
  • If iCloud sync was enabled, manage or delete retained cloud data through the app and your Apple account settings as needed.
  • Protect your device lock. You are responsible for content you share through screenshots or share cards.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Apple: App Store, StoreKit, optional iCloud / CloudKit, notifications, photo picker, share sheet, speech, Face ID / Touch ID.
  • Umeng+ (optional): product analytics only after you allow it.
  • Alibaba Cloud / Qwen (optional): AI polishing only after you start and confirm that feature.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your event content for ad targeting or cross-app advertising profiles.

7. Purchases

Days Count Pro is a one-time non-consumable unlock (not an auto-renewing subscription). Features may include photo backgrounds, watermark-free share cards, hidden events, and Live Activities. Exact features are shown in the app. Optional iCloud sync remains free for everyone. Payment and refunds are handled by Apple.

8. Children

Days Count is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a minor, use the app with a parent or guardian. Do not submit sensitive personal information to AI polishing.

9. Your Choices

  • View, edit, or delete events and settings in the app.
  • Revoke notification, photo, contacts, calendar, microphone, speech, or iCloud permissions in system Settings.
  • Avoid optional network features by leaving sync off, skipping purchases, declining analytics, and not using AI polishing.
  • Contact us with privacy questions using the email below.
